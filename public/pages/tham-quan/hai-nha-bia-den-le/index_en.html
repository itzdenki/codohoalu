CỐ ĐÔ HOA LƯ

Two stele houses of Lê Temple

The Temple of King Lê Đại Hành currently preserves two steles dating from the same period, the Later Lê dynasty (17th century).

The stele on the left side of the temple is titled “Trùng tu tạo tác thánh tượng tiền triều Lê Đại Hành hoàng đế bi ký tính danh” (“Stele Record, with Names, of the Restoration and Making of the Sacred Statues of Emperor Lê Đại Hành of the Former Dynasty”). It records the restoration work and the making of the sacred statues.

The border of the stele is carved with a pair of dragons facing the sun (lưỡng Long chầu nhật). Its base is sculpted in stepped tiers, looking sturdy and imposing.

The inscription records that in 1611 work began on carving three sacred statues, which were completed in 1612 to provide a place of worship. Prayers for the nation and the people were all answered, and the sage emperor’s grace brought peace to the realm.

The stele on the right side of the temple is titled “Tiền triều Lê Đại Hành hoàng đế bi ký” (“Stele Record of Emperor Lê Đại Hành of the Former Dynasty”) and rests on a fairly large tortoise. The stele is elaborately carved, with patterns on both faces. With its exquisite carving and rich range of subjects, this stele is regarded as an exemplary model of the form and design of Vietnamese steles.

The stele praises King Lê Đại Hành as a man of generous benevolence and divine martial prowess, courageous and wise, who commanded the ten armies and, holding high authority, supported the child-king; who brought sufficiency and contentment to the people in every direction and brought order to the realm… He built institutions, encouraged agriculture, performed the ritual plowing of the royal field (tịch điền), reduced rents and taxes, pardoned prisoners, established discipline, and strengthened defense… This may be called a monumental achievement for later generations to follow, a virtue that can never be forgotten through the ages… that is what is meant by merit and virtue.

Together with its distinctive decorative design, the content of the inscription lets visitors feel a heroic spirit radiating across the whole stele, to be admired by posterity for a thousand generations.
